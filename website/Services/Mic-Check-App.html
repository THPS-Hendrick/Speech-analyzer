How the Mic-Check App Scores Your Speech

Our proprietary Natural Language Processing (NLP) and acoustic engine breaks your communication down into three distinct, measurable categories.

Content (What You Say)

The substance of your speech is measured by scanning for exact pronouns and cross-referencing our custom sensory dictionaries.

Personal Balance (Target: 30-60%)

Measures storytelling. We scan for personal pronouns to ensure you are talking about specific people doing specific things, rather than abstract corporate jargon.

Visual Balance (Target: 20-50%)

Measures sensory words that paint a picture in the listener's mind. Low visual scores correlate directly with low audience retention.

Delivery (How You Sound)

Our acoustic engine processes live audio to map out your pacing, pausing, and psychoacoustic volume in exact 6dB biological tiers.

Speaking Pace (Target: 100-150 WPM)

Anything over 150 Words Per Minute triggers the listener's cognitive strain. The app tracks your absolute pacing in real-time.

Mumble Meter (Target: 3.5-4.5 SPS)

By calculating absolute Syllables Per Second (SPS), we detect when you are rushing through words or failing to enunciate clearly.

Simplicity (How Easily You Are Understood)

We calculate comprehension by averaging the Flesch, Gunning Fog, SMOG, and Coleman-Liau indices against a 10,000 common-word dictionary.

Speech Runtime (Target: 3.0s - 6.0s)

This is the most critical metric. Runtime is the average seconds you speak continuously before taking a meaningful pause (silence exceeding 0.70 seconds). Runtimes over 9 seconds indicate severe nervous rushing and a lack of breath control.

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed explanations of our core scoring metrics.

What is the Green Score?

The Green Score counts how many of the 9 core metrics hit their perfect target zone (+10 points). Each green metric adds +1 to this score. Getting all 9 greens awards a +1 bonus, resulting in a perfect 10/10.

What is a good Speaking Pace (Words/min)?

Your speaking pace is critical to being understood. The sweet spot (Green Zone) is 100-150 words per minute. Going faster risks confusing your audience, while speaking slower can lose their attention.

What does Runtime mean in public speaking?

Runtime is the average seconds you speak continuously before taking a meaningful pause. The optimal target is between 3.0 and 6.0 seconds. A high runtime indicates a lack of breath control and run-on sentences.

What is the ideal Pause Percentage?

Pause Percentage is the total amount of time you spend silent during your speech. You should aim for 15-35%. Pausing allows your audience to process the information and gives you time to think about your next point.
