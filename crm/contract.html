Public Speaking Services Agreement

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on by and between:

  • Service Provider: Tom Hendrick, located in South Australia ("Provider", "I", "me", "my")
  • Client: , located at ("Client", "you", "your")

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

The Provider agrees to deliver the coaching, drafting, assessment, and/or portal access services outlined in the Schedule of Services below. The standard of quality for all drafting and assessment services is based on the Provider's proprietary objective speech measuring tool, and services will be deemed complete when the deliverables meet the approved measurable metrics (the "Green Zone").

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

Payment Schedule: Coaching packages and portal access require 100% payment upfront prior to commencement. Drafting and custom presentation services require a 50% non-refundable deposit to secure the Provider's time, with the remaining 50% due upon delivery of the final draft.
Terms: All invoices are due within seven (7) days of receipt.
Late Fees: Overdue invoices will incur a late fee of 5% per month on the outstanding balance. The Provider reserves the right to suspend services or portal access if payments are past due.

3. CANCELLATIONS, RESCHEDULING, AND REFUNDS

Coaching Sessions: The Client must provide a minimum of twenty-four (24) hours' notice to reschedule a coaching session. Failure to provide 24 hours' notice, or failing to attend a scheduled session, will result in the forfeiture of that session.
Refunds: No refunds will be provided for "change of mind" or partially completed coaching packages. Refunds will only be issued if the Provider is unable to deliver the agreed-upon services.

4. DELIVERABLES, REVISIONS, AND SCOPE CREEP

Drafting Revisions: For presentation drafting services, the Client is entitled to two (2) rounds of revisions within a fourteen (14) day period following the delivery of the initial draft.
Scope Changes: Revisions must be based on the original brief. If the Client requests a complete change of topic, requires revisions beyond the included two rounds, or requests work outside the agreed Schedule of Services, such work will be billed at the Provider’s standard hourly rate stated below.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Provider’s IP: The Provider retains 100% ownership of all proprietary methodologies, speech measuring tools, rubrics, metrics, course materials, frameworks, and login portal infrastructure. The Client is granted a limited, non-exclusive license to use these tools during the term of this Agreement.
Client’s IP: Upon receipt of full payment, the Client will retain full ownership of the final customized presentation drafts and their individual assessment reports created under this Agreement.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to a mutual standard of confidentiality. The Provider will not disclose any of the Client's sensitive business information, personal stories, corporate strategies, or trade secrets shared during coaching or drafting. In return, the Client agrees not to share, distribute, or resell their portal login credentials, or the Provider’s proprietary coaching frameworks and course materials, to any third parties.

7. GUARANTEES AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Deliverable Guarantee: The Provider guarantees that the services and deliverables will be measured against and meet the objective metrics ("Green Zone") established by the Provider's speech measuring tool.
Outcome Disclaimer: The Client acknowledges that the Provider does not and cannot guarantee specific real-world outcomes resulting from the use of these services (e.g., winning a pitch, securing funding, obtaining a promotion).
Liability Cap: The Provider’s total liability for any claim arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Client to the Provider under this Agreement.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing fourteen (14) days' written notice to the other party. Upon early termination by the Client, the Client will be billed on a pro-rata basis for all work completed up to the date of termination. Any unearned deposit funds remaining after accounting for completed work will be refunded to the Client.

9. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of South Australia. Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved in the courts of South Australia.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior proposals, negotiations, understandings, and agreements, whether written or oral.

11. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

The Provider acts as an independent contractor under this Agreement. Nothing contained herein shall be construed to create an employer-employee, partnership, or joint venture relationship between the parties.

12. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing their obligations (except for payment obligations) where such failure or delay results from causes beyond their reasonable control, including but not natural disasters, global health emergencies, or severe infrastructure outages.

13. SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such provision will be struck out and the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

In the event of a dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, both parties agree to attempt to resolve the dispute through good-faith mediation before resorting to formal litigation.

15. AMENDMENTS

This Agreement may only be amended, modified, or supplemented by an agreement in writing signed by both the Provider and the Client.

16. SURVIVAL

Clauses regarding Intellectual Property, Confidentiality, Limitation of Liability, and any other provisions that by their nature should survive termination, shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement.
